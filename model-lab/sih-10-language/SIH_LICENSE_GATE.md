# License Gate

An artifact is **APPROVED FOR PROTOTYPE** only after code, weights, tokenizer/frontend, voice/dataset, redistribution, attribution, and commercial-use terms are recorded with revision and checksum.

- **CONDITIONAL:** license appears compatible but provenance, export, or Android package evidence is incomplete.
- **NON-COMPLIANT:** non-commercial, research-only, or incompatible redistribution terms, including known CC-BY-NC MMS TTS records.
- **UNKNOWN:** any missing weight or dataset/voice term.

Public GitHub/Hugging Face availability is not approval. UNKNOWN and NON-COMPLIANT artifacts must not enter Vokie production.
