# Prioritized Implementation Roadmap

## P0 — Blocking SIH requirements
1. Per-language STT/TTS artifact, license, checksum, Android ARM64, and footprint gate for all ten languages.
2. Integrate only approved languages; prove offline STT → transport → TTS on two phones.
3. Establish WER and human intelligibility methodology/corpus.

## P1 — Highest SIH score impact
1. Low/mid-range device PSS, CPU, thermal, app/model storage, STT/TTS latency and RTF measurement.
2. Noisy pause/VAD and rescue terminology evaluation.
3. T0–T8 synchronized two-phone latency protocol.

## P2 — Complete mandatory demo
1. Continuous phone-like turn manager/sentence emission design.
2. Android-safe SOS playback/volume behavior validation.
3. Embedded-device interoperability decision and adapter only if required.

## P3 — High-value Vokie differentiation
1. Physical multi-peer and reconnection validation.
2. Pair-calibrated qualitative distance validation with two phones.

## P4 — Research
Direction policy, adaptive ranging, noise enhancement, semantic/acoustic tokens, JSCC.

## P5 — Optional
Only pursue advanced research after P0/P1 evidence supports a reliable SIH demo.
