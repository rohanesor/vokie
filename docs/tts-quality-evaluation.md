# TTS quality evaluation record

## Result: NOT RUN

No candidate has a legal, complete, Android-testable artifact set. No generated audio or human ratings are presented as evidence.

## Mandatory evaluation after artifact approval

Each of Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Odia, Bengali, and English must be evaluated using native-language, linguist-reviewed versions of these semantic categories:

1. Please send help immediately.
2. We are inside the building near the main entrance.
3. There are five people with me.
4. Move to the nearest safe location.
5. There are three injured people.
6. Representative Indian names and locations.

For every backend, precision, and language, at least two qualified native listeners must independently score intelligibility, pronunciation, emergency clarity, naturalness, prosody, artifacts, and synthesis failure. Results must retain model URL/revision/checksum, speaker/style, sentence identifier, device/build, and audio duration. Audio remains under ignored `.research/` unless a later explicit retention decision is made.

## Gate

No architecture may claim superiority over MMS, acceptable emergency clarity, or production readiness until blinded native-listener results and Android-device results exist for the same corpus.
